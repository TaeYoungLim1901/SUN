# Q6 model selection: val≁test (adv AUC=1) + SSE in a minority tail. RMSE; pub=priv=½ of same 3868.
**1. IW for selection.** IWCV is unbiased for test risk iff p_te≪p_val and w=p_te/p_val is known https://jmlr.org/papers/v8/sugiyama07a.html
Adv AUC=1 ⇒ 0-error separator ⇒ supports disjoint or w unbounded (Radon–Nikodym does not exist). Cortes Fig.1: σ_Q/σ_P=0.3 (E[w²]=∞) IW error ~0.8 at n=5000; at 0.75 (E[w²]<∞) it converges https://papers.nips.cc/paper/4156-learning-bounds-for-importance-weighting
A 20-bin histogram on one coordinate is the *marginal* ratio, not p_te(x)/p_val(x). Estimating p and q separately (hist/KDE) is what Kanamori–Hido–Sugiyama reject in high-d; uLSIF estimates the ratio directly https://jmlr.org/papers/v10/kanamori09a.html
ESS≈(∑w)²/∑w²; few large bins ⇒ ESS≪n, IW variance explodes https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.03572
Clip w∈[0.2,5] is Cortes’ cap: capping the top 1% *hurt* when large weights were the true ratio, not noise https://papers.nips.cc/paper/4156-learning-bounds-for-importance-weighting
If w must be used unbounded: flatten (AIWERM w^γ γ<1; IWERM “not efficient and rather unstable”) https://doi.org/10.1016/S0378-3758(00)00115-4 or RuLSIF relative ratio p_te/(α p_te+(1-α)p_val), always bounded https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.4729
**2. Stratified / tail score (the established substitute).** CVaR_α(ℓ)=sup{E_Q ℓ: Q≪P, dQ/dP≤1/α}=worst-group risk over every mass-α set; for SE it *is* mean SE on the worst α-fraction https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.08750 https://doi.org/10.21314/JOR.2000.038
Group DRO: select on worst-group loss not average; +10–40 pp on the tail, average held https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.08731 — WILDS: under real shift report worst-group not average https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.07421
Kaggle fix when adv AUC≫0.5: sort labeled rows by p(is-test), take the most test-like slice as *new* val. Numerai AUC 0.87: poly-LR won ordinary val, lost public; rebuilt val matched private (0.6922/0.6958 → 0.6916/0.6954) https://fastml.com/adversarial-validation-part-two
Gulrajani–Lopez-Paz: training-domain val assumes P_tr≈P_te; if false the algorithm is incomplete. Use leave-one-domain-out, or test-domain val with a **20-query cap** (no early-stop on it) https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.01434
If no val row has p(is-test)>½, FastML has nothing to pick — IW and rebuilt-val both empty; the only unbiased probe of P_te is public LB https://fastml.com/adversarial-validation-part-two
**3. Winners when “better overall CV” ≠ “better on the mass that is the metric.”** Adv AUC≈0.5: trust CV (Santander val RF 75.3 ↔ priv 74.4) https://fastml.com/adversarial-validation-part-one
Adv AUC high: ordinary CV is the *wrong ranking*. Numerai better-CV poly lost LB; rebuilt-val fixed it https://fastml.com/adversarial-validation-part-two
Psychopathy: CV rose while private fell; pub #2 → priv #52; CV–private scatter flat; first two submissions were best and were **not** selected https://gregpark.io/blog/Kaggle-Psychopathy-Postmortem
ASHRAE RMSE time-split: pub #1 → #497 after dropping dew-temp *because CV improved without it* https://www.kaggle.com/c/ashrae-energy-prediction/discussion/122796 — 3rd: the method they thought overfit (worse CV) was best private https://www.kaggle.com/c/ashrae-energy-prediction/discussion/124984
TPS Nov 2021 (adv-separable): “CV useless — trust public LB” https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/tabular-playground-series-nov-2021/writeups/ambrosm-3-solution-don-t-trust-the-cv-scores
Porto (AUC≈0.5): CV tracked *private*, public did not; chasing public cost gold https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/porto-seguro-safe-driver-prediction/writeups/dream-of-gini-keep-it-simple-21st-solution-mea-cul
Optiver RMSPE: 1st public → 154 private after a size-feature shift adv-val had flagged https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/optiver-realized-volatility-prediction/writeups/kaggle-is-hard-my-journey-1st-public-154-private
This contest is AmbrosM not Porto: pub and priv are a split of the *same* 3868, so public is exchangeable with private and val is not. When overall-val Δ and public Δ disagree, public ranks P_te. Pick the card that wins the group holding SSE (worst 5%/y>550), not lower overall val RMSE https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/tabular-playground-series-nov-2021/writeups/ambrosm-3-solution-don-t-trust-the-cv-scores https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.08731
**4. How many (CV,LB) pairs for a fitted slope.** OLS: 10–15 obs **per parameter** (intercept+a ⇒ n≳20–30 *same-axis* pairs) https://www.revespcardiol.org/en-regression-modeling-strategies-articulo-S1885585711002726
SE(a)=σ/(s_x √n); n=6 has df=4 — a 6-pair fit flipped 2.08↔0.768 on one point; n=11 still resid σ=0.199. Blum–Hardt: Kaggle-style public error grows as **√k** in adaptive submissions; you cannot keep adding pairs and refit https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.04585
Dwork et al.: each adaptive look at a holdout leaks; after ~√n queries it is spent (n_pub≈1934 ⇒ ~44 *LB reads*, but a 2-parameter slope is estimated from the ~10 *pairs*, not 1934 rows) https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.2664
Cawley–Talbot: repeated selection on the same val overfits by an amount comparable to algorithm differences https://jmlr.org/papers/v11/cawley10a.html
Gulrajani oracle = 20 queries, no peeking for early-stop https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.01434
**8 slots left: do not fit a new CV→LB slope.** Clone-swaps and architecture-adds are different mechanisms; mixing them is a misspecified regression. Pre-register one selector (CVaR_{0.05} / RMSE|y>550, or FastML test-like val if any row has p>½). Burn 1–2 slots as confirmatory public tests of that selector, not as more (x,y) points https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.04585 https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.01434
